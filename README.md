# 🚀 Project Atlas

A consulting-grade enterprise transformation project focused on designing a complete transformation strategy for a large e-commerce organization using industry-standard Project Management and Business Analysis practices.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

Project Atlas aims to develop a comprehensive transformation roadmap that helps a large e-commerce organization improve:

- 🟦 People & Culture
- 🟨 Operational Excellence
- 🟩 Digital & AI
- 🟪 Customer Experience
- 🟧 Strategic Growth & Governance

---

## 🗺️ Transformation Journey

```
People & Culture
        ↓
Operational Excellence
        ↓
Digital & AI
        ↓
Customer Experience
        ↓
Strategic Growth & Governance
```
